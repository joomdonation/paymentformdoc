### Chapter 3

# Frontend Display

Choose what you want to show on your Joomla site.

- [Display a List of Forms](/frontend-items/display-forms) so visitors can choose a form.
- [Display a Single Form](/frontend-items/display-a-single-form) on its own page, in an article, or in a module position.
- [Display Payment History](/frontend-items/payment-history) so signed-in users can see the payment records associated with their account.
