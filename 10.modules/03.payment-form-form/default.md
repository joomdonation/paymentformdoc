Use **Payment Form - Form** to let visitors complete one form directly on an existing page. Select the form under **Form** in the module editor. Set its layout, fields, and payment methods in the Payment Form component.

For the setup steps, including position and menu assignment, see [Show a form in a module position](/frontend-items/display-a-single-form#show-a-form-in-a-module-position).
