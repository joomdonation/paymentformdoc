title: Modules
---

Payment Form installs two Joomla site modules. Use them to display a form or links to forms in a position provided by your site template.

| What you want to show | Module |
| --- | --- |
| A form visitors can fill in on the current page | [Payment Form - Form](/modules/payment-form-form) |
| A list of links visitors can follow to open individual forms | [Payment Form - Forms List](/modules/payment-form-forms-list) |

For a dedicated page or an embed within a Joomla article, see [Frontend Display](/frontend-items).
