Use [Manage Payments on the Site](/frontend-management/manage-payments) to give staff access to payment records without granting access to the Joomla administrator area. Joomla access levels control who can see the page, and Payment Form permissions control what they can do there.

For customers who only need their own records and invoices, add a [Payment History](/frontend-items/payment-history) page. For staff who already work in the Joomla administrator area, see [Payments Management](/payments-management).
