### Chapter 2

# Form Setup

Start with what the form needs to collect and how it should calculate the amount. Then add the fields, choose a payment method if needed, and publish the form on your site.

| What you want to do | Where to start |
| --- | --- |
| Collect information only, charge a fixed amount, accept donations, or calculate a price from form inputs | [Form Types](/basic-setup/forms/form-types) |
| Add fields, show follow-up questions, or charge for selected options | [Custom Fields](/basic-setup/custom-fields) |
| Change columns, field widths, or label placement | [Modern Form Layouts](/basic-setup/forms/modern-form-layouts) |
| Accept a bank transfer or connect an online gateway | [Payment Plugins](/basic-setup/payment-plugins) |
| Offer a discount code | [Coupons](/basic-setup/coupons) |
| Add a fixed tax rate or calculate EU VAT from the payer's country | [Taxes and EU VAT](/basic-setup/eu-tax-rules) |
| Require customers to accept a privacy policy | [Privacy Policy Checkbox](/basic-setup/gdpr) |
| Translate forms, messages, or interface labels | [Translation](/basic-setup/translation) |

Use [Configuration](/getting-started/configuration) for site-wide defaults such as currency, payment emails, and PDF invoices. When your form is ready, [display it on your site](/frontend-items) and [test a submission from start to finish](/getting-started/test-a-form).
