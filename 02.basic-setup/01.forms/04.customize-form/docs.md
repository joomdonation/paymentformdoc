## Registration and login

In Joomla, open **Components → Payment Form → Configuration**.

- Set **User Registration Integration** to **Yes** to let users create an account while completing a form.
- Set **Show Login Box** to **Yes** to let users with an existing account sign in before submitting the form. Payment Form associates the resulting payment record with their account.

## Form fields

Go to **Components → Payment Form → Custom Fields** to publish or unpublish fields, mark fields as required, change their titles, or add fields. Payment Form includes core fields such as **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email**.

See [Custom Fields](/basic-setup/custom-fields) for field setup and [Custom Fee Fields](/basic-setup/custom-fields/custom-fee-fields) to change the amount based on the user's selections.
